Geotechnical Investigation

We perform field investigations for all types of structures — from individual homes to large-scale expressways and metro infrastructure. Our team works across India with precision and speed. We perform all types of geotechnical testing across diverse environments, including land, marine, mountain terrains, forest, river, and marshy sites. Our investigations include detailed soil profiling, groundwater level assessment, and seismic hazard evaluation. We use advanced geophysical methods and provide comprehensive reports with maps, cross-sections, and engineering recommendations.

Soil Mechanics Lab

We are associated with NABL-accredited laboratories and educational institutes to perform comprehensive soil testing. Every test follows IS standards, ensuring accuracy and repeatability. We offer complete testing services for physical, mechanical, and chemical properties of soil. Our lab services include advanced testing for soil behavior under various conditions, including cyclic loading and environmental effects. We provide detailed analysis for foundation design, slope stability, and earthwork projects.
