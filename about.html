About VGS

Founded in 2016, Vindhyan Geotechnical Services is India's trusted partner for Soil Investigation & Geotechnical Engineering.

Who We Are

Built on Trust,
Accuracy & Reliability

Founded by Mr. Sankatha Tiwari in 2016, Vindhyan Geotechnical Services has grown steadily to become one of India's recognized leaders in Geotechnical Engineering Services.

We excel in Soil Investigation and have executed projects pan-India across industrial, residential, commercial, and infrastructure sectors. Our in-house expertise and NABL-accredited lab associations ensure every solution is accurate and cost-effective.

We deliver on schedule and within budget — every time.

Our Vision

To be a strong multi-faceted corporate committed to Professionalism, Quality, On-time project completion, and Customer satisfaction — exceeding expectations through standardized, best-practice methodologies.

Our Mission

To provide cost-effective, timely geotechnical and civil engineering solutions with the highest level of professionalism by adopting modern techniques and accepted standard solutions.

Our Edge

Experienced staff and consultants with national and international project exposure. We solve site-specific engineering problems with proven, practical methodologies tailored to every client.
